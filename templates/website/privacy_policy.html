Privacy Policy

This page explains what data this site collects, why, and how it's handled.

Who this applies to

This site (andreeastech.pythonanywhere.com) is operated by Andreea Sandu. This policy covers the personal data submitted through the Contact, Start a Project, and Reviews forms.

What data is collected

  • Contact form: name, email address, and message.
  • Start a Project form: name, email address, industry, project description, hosting details, timeline, and requested features.
  • Reviews form: name, company (optional), rating, and review message.

No payment, financial, or government ID data is collected through this site.

Why it's collected

Submitted data is used only to respond to your inquiry, evaluate a project brief, or — for reviews — to consider it for publication on the Reviews page. It is not used for advertising and is not sold or shared with third parties, other than the email service used to deliver notifications to the site owner.

Published reviews

Reviews are private until manually approved. Once approved, the name, company (if provided), rating, and message you submitted are displayed publicly on the Reviews page.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies strictly necessary for it to function — a session cookie and a CSRF-protection cookie. No analytics or advertising cookies are used.

How long data is kept

Submitted data is kept for as long as needed to respond to your request, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the data you've submitted at any time. To do so, get in touch through the Contact page.
