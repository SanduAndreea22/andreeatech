The customer's story

A phone that rings exactly when the kitchen is at its busiest, a reservation scrambled onto a scrap of paper that gets lost among the orders, a customer who arrives at the door and finds out their table "got lost somewhere." Every one of these moments is a customer who could have come back, but leaves unhappy instead.

You don't need another static website with a hard-to-read PDF menu. You need a site that shows your menu clearly, takes reservations automatically, and gives you your time back — time you can spend on the dining experience, not on admin work.

Why me

I don't build a generic website later adapted for restaurants — I understand the flow of a busy dining room and what happens when the booking system can't keep up with reality: double-booked tables, lost orders, unhappy customers at the door. The system I build for you is designed to eliminate exactly these moments, not just to look good.
