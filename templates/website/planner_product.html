Why you need it

Why the usual planner lets you down exactly on the hard days

You've probably already tried a classic planner or a task app. It works well when you have energy. But on the days when you're overwhelmed or tired, that same rigid system becomes extra pressure.

Most planners treat every day the same. Emotional Planner starts from the opposite place: before the list, you note down your state and energy. Then you choose what's realistic for the day you actually have, not the day you think you should be having.

At the end, two simple questions help you close the day with clarity: what drained you and what small win you had. Over time, you start seeing patterns, not just unchecked tasks.

You don't have to be productive every day for the system to work. You just have to be honest with yourself, once a day.

The product's story

I turned a hard period into a product that makes room for you.

The idea was born during a period when a list of goals stopped helping. Writing became a way to bring order to my thoughts, without the pressure of fixing everything in a single day.

As a developer, I started from a simple question: if a problem can be understood, a solution can be built around it too. That's how a planner was born that tracks both the time and the state behind it.

Today, Personal Balance & Productivity Planner is a digital space for the good days, the full days, and the days when you need to take it slower.
